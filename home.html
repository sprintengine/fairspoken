Recognition

Runs the Whisper engine on the GPU (whisper builds only); turn off to force CPU.
Also runs a Whisper model on the same audio and keeps Parakeet's text except where Whisper spells one of your dictionary terms. That is roughly twice the speech work and the Whisper model's memory on top of Parakeet's, so expect more battery drain. Auto runs it only on mains power. Install the Whisper model in Models first.
Extra memory while dictating: Tiny about 0.2 GB, Base about 0.3 GB, Small about 0.8 GB, Large v3 Turbo about 2 GB.

Formatting

Polish lays out what you say for where you say it: greeting and paragraphs in email, short lines in chat, lists in notes. It only moves your words and never adds a greeting or sign-off you did not say.

When no built-in rule knows a website or app, ask the polish model once which format fits.
A website such as example.com, or an app as app: and its bundle id.
