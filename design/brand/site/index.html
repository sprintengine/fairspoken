01

How it works

Three steps. You only do the first.

  1. 01SpeakHold fn anywhere and talk the way you'd talk to a colleague. Ramble, pause, change your mind.
  2. 02SignalLet go. NVIDIA Parakeet transcribes on the Apple Neural Engine in about 40–80 ms. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. 03Clean textSpeakoFlow Mini, a small clean-up model trained on dictation, drops the ums, settles "no wait, I meant…" and types the result at your cursor.

03

Privacy

Runs on hardware you own.

Speech is turned into text on your Mac, or on a host you run on your own network.

  • Processed on siteOn your Mac, or on one Fairspoken host in the back office. No cloud speech service is involved unless you deliberately switch one on.
  • Encrypted between your own devicesPractice devices reach the host over Tailscale, which encrypts every connection end to end with WireGuard.
  • Private on screen, tooHide dictation previews with one click when a patient is in the room. You choose how long history is kept.
  • Checkable, not just promisedEvery line is open source under the MIT licence, so your DPO, or anyone they trust, can confirm it.
04

The practice host

One Mac mini. Every room.

Run Fairspoken host on a Mac mini in the back office. Every Mac, Windows PC, iPad and phone in the practice dictates through it, so each room gets Neural Engine speed without a Mac on every desk.

host online12 dictations / minp50 61 ms

Set up once, before the morning list

Install the host, sign the practice devices into your tailnet and pair them. About fifteen minutes, start to finish.

Scales with the practice

Add workers for a busy clinic, keep a second model loaded, and watch queue time and latency per room as it happens.

Works on the PCs you already have

The Windows and Linux app can stream audio straight to your host. Nothing new to buy for the consulting rooms.

For developers

Talk to your agents at the speed you think.

Your voice is a signal; your agents need clean bits. Fairspoken sits between the two and throws away the noise, so Claude Code, Cursor and your terminal get exactly what you meant.

Dictated in 41 s · polished on site

What your practice manager and DPO will ask, answered.

Where does the audio go?

To the Mac in the room, or to your own Fairspoken host in the building, over an encrypted Tailscale connection. In local and host mode it isn't sent to us or to any cloud speech service. The optional cloud service is off unless someone turns it on, and practices can leave it off.

What's kept, and for how long?

Audio is used to produce the text and isn't sent anywhere else. The text history stays on the device for as long as you choose, and you can clear it at any time.

Will it get Irish names and places right?

Add your names to the vocabulary list, from Ó Súilleabháin and Ní Bhriain to Dún Laoghaire and Clonakilty, along with your consultants, and Fairspoken spells them correctly from then on.

Does it work with our clinical system?

It types wherever your cursor is, so it works with any practice management system, browser or email client without an integration.

What does it cost?

The app and host are free and open source. The only hardware a practice might add is one Mac mini to act as the host.
